小识谱隐私政策

开发者 simonsland · 生效日期 2026 年 11 月 24 日

练习时会保存什么？

小识谱在你的设备上记录练习时间、题目、按键作答、正确率和反应时间，用于展示历史、计算统计和安排针对性练习。练习设置、音色、声音和触感偏好也保存在本机，以便下次使用。

练习无需注册或使用麦克风。小识谱不会将练习记录或偏好发送给开发者或第三方，也不展示广告或追踪你在其他 App 和网站上的活动。

如何删除数据？

练习记录保留至你主动删除。在「设置 → 隐私 → 删除所有练习数据」中确认后，本机的练习历史、答题明细和相关统计将被清除。要一并清除本机偏好，请在系统中选择「删除 App」。如果你开启了设备备份，备份中可能含有 App 数据，你可以在相应的备份设置中管理副本。

如何联系开发者？

如对本政策有一般性问题或建议，可以在小识谱的 GitHub 问题页留言。留言和 GitHub 用户名会公开显示，请勿提交联系方式、练习记录或其他私人信息。清除练习记录可直接在 App 内完成。

访问本页与政策更新

本页由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问时，GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。政策更新后，我们会在此发布新版本并标明生效日期。

PiaNotes Privacy Policy

Developer: simonsland · Effective November 24, 2026

What does PiaNotes save during practice?

PiaNotes stores session times, exercises, key presses, accuracy, and response times on your device to show your history, calculate statistics, and suggest focused exercises. It saves your exercise settings, piano sound, audio preferences, and haptic preferences locally for your next session.

You can practice without an account or microphone access. PiaNotes does not send practice records or preferences to the developer or third parties, show ads, or track your activity across other apps or websites.

How can you delete your data?

Practice records are kept until you delete them. Confirm Settings → Privacy → Delete all practice data to clear local practice history, individual answers, and related statistics. To clear local preferences as well, choose Delete App in your device settings. If device backups are enabled, they may include app data. You can manage those copies in your backup settings.

How can you contact the developer?

For general questions or suggestions about this policy, visit the PiaNotes issue tracker on GitHub. Your post and GitHub username will be public. Please leave out contact details, practice records, and other private information. You can delete practice records directly in the app.

Visiting this page and policy updates

GitHub Pages hosts this page. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. We will publish updates on this page and include the date each version takes effect.
